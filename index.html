Before you buy

Research the car, not just the price.

Recalls, complaints and fuel cost

Public records for the model year, in one place.

  • NHTSA recall campaigns, complaints and crash ratings
  • EPA fuel-cost research
  • A link to NHTSA’s lookup for the actual VIN

Model-year records do not establish this VIN’s recall repair status or safety. Check the actual VIN with NHTSA and the manufacturer.

Vehicle history reports

Optional VINData reports, bought one at a time or as a 5-pack — with or without Pro.

  • Reported title brands, salvage and total-loss records
  • Odometer readings and open liens
  • Recovered thefts and other reported events, where available

Active theft records, detailed accident or repair history and auction photos are not provided. Missing records do not mean a clean title or a safe car. Reports need sign-in and your approval to send the VIN to VINData. Every report includes the NMVTIS disclaimer and is no substitute for an inspection.

Straight talk

What CurbWhiz won’t do.

It is a calculator and a checklist. The judgment — and the inspection — stay with you.

  • No market values, no listing discovery. The numbers come from what you enter.
  • No promise of profit. Results depend on reviewed inputs and available evidence.
  • No verdict on the car. “Meets your target” is about the money, not a recommendation to buy.
  • Not financial, legal or tax advice. Check tax, title and fee rules with your state and the seller or auction.

Questions

Asked before the auction.

The short answers. The support guide has the rest.

Open the support guide
Do I need an account?

No account is needed for local checks or worked examples. Sign-in is needed for vehicle history reports and account features. Signing in alone does not upload or synchronize deals.

Where do the numbers come from?

From what you enter. CurbWhiz does the arithmetic: fees, tax, repairs, contingency, margin and the highest price that fits. It doesn’t look up market values or find listings. For a real auction, enter the buyer fees from its own terms.

Why does a cost say “Unknown” instead of $0?

Because you haven’t entered it yet, and CurbWhiz never treats a missing cost as free. Fill in the line it names, or mark it “Not applicable” if it really doesn’t apply.

Is the result a recommendation to buy?

No. “Meets your target under these assumptions” is about the money, given the numbers you entered. It isn’t a judgment about the car. The checks section lists what to confirm — title, VIN, inspection, mileage and prices — before you buy.

Does it work offline?

Local deal calculations and saved content on your device work offline. Sign-in, subscription changes, fresh VIN decoding, new history reports, cloud AI and device sync need a connection.

Where are my deals stored?

On your device. If you explicitly enable device sync, selected deals can also be stored in your private CurbWhiz account. Signing in alone does not upload them.
